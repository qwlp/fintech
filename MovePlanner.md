**Calculation Part:** 

**Total First-Year Cost  \=**  
Total \= Flights \+ Visa Package \+ Tuition \+ Accommodation \+ Insurance \+ Books Equipment \+ Settling In \+ Living Expenses \+ Contingency

Where:

•  Visa Package \= Visa Fee \+ Hold Fund \+ Immigration Health Surcharge (or residence permit) \+ Medical Exam \+ Biometrics

•  Accommodation \= (Deposit × 1–2 months) \+ (Monthly Rent × Academic Months)

•  Living Expenses \= Daily Expenses × Academic Days  OR  Monthly Living × Academic Months

•  Contingency \= 0.10 × (sum of all above)   // 10% recommended

\-\> **Cost-to-Arrive (sponsor view)** \= Flights \+ Visa Package \+ First Month Accom \+ Settling In \+ First Month Living \+ Deposit 

**Daily\_Expenses \= Food \+ Local\_Transport \+ Phone\_Internet \+ Laundry \+ Personal\_Misc \+ Social\_Buffer**
