# Five-minute demonstration

Use a dedicated student account with fictional data. Confirm sandbox labels remain visible. Load the scenario before recording; save the tuition deadline and reserve, choose an eligible route, confirm the fixture recipient, and verify the AI service is configured. Refresh quotes immediately before demonstrating preparation.

| Time      | Demonstration                                                    | Evidence                                                              |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0:00–0:35 | Student has tuition to pay while protecting living costs         | Study plan and sandbox USD balance                                    |
| 0:35–1:15 | Compare routes through AI against the saved deadline             | Recorded costs, eligible recommendation, buffered arrival and reserve |
| 1:15–1:45 | Stress-test a 10% adverse currency move                          | Hypothetical loss and remaining valuation                             |
| 1:45–2:25 | Ask AI to prepare tuition payment                                | Exact proposal; no debit yet                                          |
| 2:25–3:15 | Review recipient, quote, expiry and reserve; approve and execute | Unique receipt and simulated balance debit                            |
| 3:15–4:00 | Download evidence and inspect security audit                     | Preparation, approval, execution and ledger                           |
| 4:00–4:40 | Show deadline, stale-quote, unauthorized and retry protections   | Named test cases, not fabricated live errors                          |
| 4:40–5:00 | Explain sponsor privacy and limitations                          | Purpose-limited dashboard; fixtures rather than live quotes           |

Suggested prompt: “Compare my tuition routes against my saved deadline and explain the eligible recommendation and protected living-cost reserve.”

Show the chat’s recorded calculation details. Briefly tighten the deadline or raise the reserve in Payments to show a blocked route, then restore the saved plan before preparing. If a source quote expires, refresh it before asking for preparation.

Then: “What would a 10% drop in my home currencies do to my available funds?”

Then: “Prepare my tuition payment using my selected route.”

The user approves in Payments. Never narrate the AI as approving or moving money by itself. Label local-test evidence separately from authenticated development-session evidence. A model-service failure may occur after successful preparation: check Payments before retrying and show the recoverable state honestly.
