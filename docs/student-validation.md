# International-student validation protocol

This is a study plan. No participants or usability results are claimed.

Recruit 5–8 currently enrolled international students with informed participation. Use fictional balances and example university details; never collect passwords, bank credentials or real invoices. Ask permission separately for screen recording and quote use. Allow participants to withdraw and remove identifying information from submission materials.

## Tasks

1. Plan a £2,000 tuition payment while protecting £1,000 for living costs. Set a deadline and select an eligible route.
2. Tighten the deadline so the cheaper route becomes unsuitable. Explain which route can still meet the plan and why arrival remains an estimate.
3. Interpret a funding shortfall. Distinguish minimum additional USD, a sponsor promise and available wallet cash.
4. Ask the assistant to compare, then prepare. Review recipient, total, expiry, deadline and reserve before approving the simulated payment.
5. Retrieve the receipt and calculation evidence. Explain what the AI can do and what requires owner approval.

## Record actual measurements

For each task record completion, time in seconds, moderator interventions, incorrect actions, and participant confidence on a 1–5 scale. Ask the participant whether remaining GBP includes the reserve, whether a quoted instruction should authorize preparation, and whether sandbox recipient confirmation is real bank verification.

Compare against a simple spreadsheet/manual route comparison with the same fictional inputs. Counterbalance task order. Report the sample size, recruitment method, observed median times, exact completion counts and limitations. Keep individual observations anonymized. Do not generalize a small convenience sample to all students.

## Submission evidence

Include a consented anonymized observation table, clearly labeled participant quotations, changed design decisions, and an authenticated app demo. Keep research consent forms private. A written protocol is not proof of user validation.
